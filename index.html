Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of Audio Signal Processing and Multimodal Machine Learning, with a focus on understanding the semantic and spatial properties of complex acoustic environments.

My research spans three interconnected directions:

  • Semantic Audio Understanding: Robust speech and audio analysis in complex acoustic environments, including target speaker extraction and pathological speech detection.
  • Spatial Audio Modeling: Sound source localization, spatially conditioned extraction, and spatial audio rendering under diverse acoustic and microphone configurations.
  • Spatial-Semantic Scene Understanding: Integrating semantic and spatial information for higher-level acoustic scene understanding, including spatial audio question answering.

More broadly, I have deep interest in the following fields:

  • Physics-Informed Machine Learning
  • Electroencephalography (EEG) Brain Signal Processing
